# Chemistry sound recordings

`water-pour.wav` is derived from **Pouring Water into a Glass Bowl.wav** by
**The_Runner_01**:

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/The_Runner_01/sounds/554444/
- Download: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/554/554444_4688703-hq.mp3
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal**, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Edits: continuous excerpt from 29.00–42.00 seconds, stereo 44,100 Hz / 16-bit PCM,
  120 ms loop crossfade, peak normalized to 80% of full scale.
- Playback: 12.88 seconds of recorded water flow at its original speed and pitch.
  Only volume follows the beaker's flow; the loop is needed only for extended pours.

The recording is bundled locally; playback does not contact Freesound.
